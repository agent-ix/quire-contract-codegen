# Quarantined pre-exit-status evidence

These records were produced before the collector retained numeric exit statuses for every command.
Their envelopes therefore cannot substantiate their historical `conclusive` result claims and MUST
NOT be imported as authoritative assurance evidence.

They remain only as development history. The authoritative evidence verifier deliberately scans
only direct `evidence/foundation-*` records and excludes this quarantine.

Quarantined records are superseded by the newest direct foundation record whose source revision is
the reviewed candidate and whose complete status census, checksums, manifest artifacts, schemas, and
envelope links pass `make verify-evidence`.
