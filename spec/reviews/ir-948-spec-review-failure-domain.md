## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. The inconclusive handling of the CoverUnsatisfied control is sound: a MemoryExhausted, timeout or unknown run is inconclusive, the peak is reported, the bound is fixed at the AC-126 ceiling and unwind 8, and nothing weaker is credited. That matters: IR-948's 2026-10-21 measurement ended this exact control Inconclusive MemoryExhausted at 17,205,592,064 B against a 17,179,869,184 B ceiling, and unwind 4 ended UnwindBoundExhausted. Checked against the IR-1436 decision comment (8f783bed, D1 and D5 adopted). Two defects against D1/D5 wording and one low on outcome vocabulary.

| FND-001 | medium | 'with FR-017-AC-63's verdict and playback taken from the witness phase' governs the whole run sentence, including the unmutated Verified runs and the CoverUnsatisfied control. Under IR-1436 D1 (adopted) and FR-017-AC-63, Verified and CoverUnsatisfied come from the proof phase (no concrete playback, --slice-formula kept), and no witness run follows them; only a Falsified verdict and its playback come from the witness phase. AC-129 also does not say that a mutant whose witness phase disagrees (WitnessDisagreed) or stops on a resource limit is inconclusive and its control unmet, which AC-126 and AC-41 do. Restrict the witness-phase clause to the two invariant mutants and list WitnessDisagreed and a witness-phase resource stop as inconclusive. | spec/kani/functional/FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md:937 |
| FND-002 | medium | 'no prepared or pre-decoded data (a clause claim is not a function body, FR-021-AC-44 to AC-47)'. The conclusion that IR-1436 D5 does not apply is right: D5 governs FR-021 function-body prepared-function const data (IR-1244), and a clause claim embeds FR-014-AC-38's rendered clause oracle. But the V2 clause generator does pre-decode the admitted package at generation time: src/oracle/clause.rs plan_clause builds a PreparedClause (lowered step plan, input names, lower/upper bounds), and the harness renders accessor ranges as constants. Read literally, the clause forbids that. No adverse control in the AC-129 mutation row tests it, so as written it cannot fail. Say instead: no FR-021 prepared-function descriptors or const data; the clause oracle and domains are generated from the strict-reader-admitted package (FR-014-AC-38, FR-015-AC-39), which is D5's admitted-package condition. | spec/kani/functional/FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md:937 |
| FND-003 | low | The inconclusive clause for the CoverUnsatisfied control does not say the control is then unmet (AC-41 does: 'the control is unmet, not weakened'), so a reader could take a reported peak as meeting it. It mixes layers: 'not ResourceExhausted' names the terminal IncompleteCause that src/kani/terminal.rs maps from both MemoryExhausted and UnwindBoundExhausted, while MemoryExhausted is a KaniInconclusiveReason. At the terminal layer CoverUnsatisfied maps to Proved with zero success checks, so the AC should name the KaniRunOutcome layer. 'unknown' names no KaniInconclusiveReason (NoVerdict, MissingCoverSummary, FailedWithoutCounterexample and VacuousProof exist). The unwind for the healthy and mutant runs is not stated. | spec/kani/functional/FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md:937 |
