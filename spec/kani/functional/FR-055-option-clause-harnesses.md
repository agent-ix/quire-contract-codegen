# FR-055: Lower option presence and value in V2 clause Kani harnesses

## Description

A V2 clause whose condition reads an option, `Option<T>` or an optional field `field?`, through
`option.present` or `option.value` is refused today as a node family with no finite encoding
(`UnsupportedObligation::NoFiniteEncoding`, FR-015-AC-62). This requirement lowers those two
operations over an option whose payload is a Boolean, a bounded integer, or a record or tuple
whose leaves are Boolean or bounded integers (a composite option), including the absent case,
and refuses every other option shape with one specific typed refusal. It extends the V2 clause
route of [FR-015](./FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md) (FR-015-AC-40, FR-015-AC-41 and the thin
`ClauseStop` of FR-015-AC-145) and creates no parallel refusal family: the new refusal is one
more arm of `UnsupportedObligation`, beside `NoFiniteEncoding` and `PathGuardNotLowered`.

Composite equality over option operands (FR-015-AC-69 to FR-015-AC-71, IR-264) is a different
family, `composite_equality`, and is untouched.

### Tractability measurement (IR-1448 probe)

The encoding below was probed before it was specified, as a hand-written scratch crate with
installed Kani 0.68.0 following the IR-1022 recipe: a native presence `bool` and payload `i64`
in a `Copy` struct, a `Copy` payload-free stop and no heap-carrying enum arm. The probe,
its limits and its verdicts are recorded in
`docs/qualification/ir1448-kani-option-probe.md`. It is a measurement of the encoding, not
evidence of CG generation: no harness was generated by CG, and no criterion below is
implemented.

## Inputs

- An admitted V2 clause claim (FR-015-AC-40) whose body reads a state field, parameter or
  result whose checked type is `Option<T>`, with `T` a Boolean, a bounded integer, or a record
  or tuple of such leaves.
- The reader-admitted option operations `option.present` and `option.value` applied to that
  operand, and the clause's other operations as FR-015-AC-40 and FR-015-AC-41 admit them.
- The proof subject, backend and ceilings of FR-015, [FR-025](./FR-025-generated-subject-abi.md)
  and [FR-028](./FR-028-bounded-proof-ceilings.md).

## Outputs

- A supported clause: its harness, symbolic-input correspondence (presence leaf, then payload
  leaves in ascending path order) and source-associated identity record.
- A refused clause: `Unsupported(OptionNotLowered { node_id, reason })` and no harness, with
  `reason` one of `PayloadNotScalar`, `NestedOption`, `NullablePresence` or
  `OptionOperationNotLowered`, each retaining the node and the offending family or operation.

## Behavior

### Draw

When a clause reads an option operand of a supported shape, the harness SHALL draw one
presence `bool` and, for each payload leaf, one native `i64` or `bool`, as plain `Copy`
values with no allocation and no heap-carrying enum arm.

When the presence draw is false, the harness SHALL constrain every payload leaf to its
canonical value (`0` for an integer leaf, `false` for a Boolean leaf), so that each abstract
option value corresponds to exactly one draw. When the presence draw is true, the harness
SHALL constrain each integer leaf inside its declared bound by the rule of FR-015-AC-11.

### Operations

When a clause applies `option.present`, the oracle SHALL return the presence draw.

When a clause applies `option.value` to a present option, the oracle SHALL return the payload
(a composite payload is projected leaf by leaf through the clause's own field or position
reads).

When a clause applies `option.value` to an absent option, the oracle SHALL stop with the
existing payload-free `ClauseStop::Undefined`, the outcome the quire-exact option kernel reports
for that read (the kernel outcome, not this text, is the authority), and the harness SHALL NOT substitute a default
value, a canonical payload or an assumption that excludes the absent draw.

When a clause guards `option.value` with `option.present` through a short-circuit connective
(`implies`, `and`, `or`) the oracle SHALL evaluate the guarded operand only when the
connective's left operand requires it, as the scalar clause oracle already does for
`implies`; the absent draw SHALL therefore complete without a stop for a guarded read.

### Refusal

When an option operand has a payload that is not a Boolean, a bounded integer or a record or
tuple of those leaves (a text, decimal, rational, float, quantity, enum or collection leaf),
CG SHALL refuse the clause as `OptionNotLowered` with reason `PayloadNotScalar` naming the
leaf family. An option of an unbounded integer keeps `requires_bound` (FR-015-AC-61); it is
not remapped to `PayloadNotScalar`.

When an option operand is itself the payload of an option, CG SHALL refuse it with reason
`NestedOption`.

When a record field of optional presence needs the third `null` state of FR-015-AC-70
(distinct from `absent`), CG SHALL refuse the clause with reason `NullablePresence`.

When a clause applies an option operation other than `option.present` and `option.value`,
CG SHALL refuse it with reason `OptionOperationNotLowered` naming the operation.

The refusals SHALL NOT map to `NoFiniteEncoding`, `StateFrameRefused` or `RequiresBound`
(the last except for the unbounded integer above), and a refused item SHALL NOT discard a
supported sibling (FR-015-AC-23).

### Evidence

The harness identity record SHALL retain the option encoding, the presence leaf and the
payload leaves with their canonical-absent rule, so that a change of encoding changes the
identity. Counterexample playback SHALL decode a presence leaf and its payload leaves into
one option witness value and SHALL refuse a playback whose absent option carries a
non-canonical payload.

## Acceptance Criteria

All controls use admitted source-backed V2 inputs. Every healthy proof is paired with a
mutant that must fail at a named step on a named distinguishing input. No criterion below is
implemented, and the probe record is not CG evidence.

| ID | Criteria | Verification |
