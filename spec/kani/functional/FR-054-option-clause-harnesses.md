# FR-054: Lower option presence and value in V2 clause Kani harnesses

## Description

A V2 clause whose body applies `quire.op.option.present` or `quire.op.option.value` is refused
today as `UnsupportedObligation::V2ClauseOperatorNotSupported` (FR-015-AC-42), because the V2
clause arm admits only connectives, the six integer comparisons and integer add, subtract,
multiply and negate. This requirement lowers those two operations over an operand whose checked
type is `Option<T>`, with `T` a Boolean, a bounded integer, or a record or tuple whose leaves are
Boolean or bounded integers (a composite option), including the absent case. It refuses every
other option shape with one specific typed refusal, and amends FR-015-AC-42 to say so
(FR-054-AC-23).

An optional record field `field?` is not lowered by this requirement. Every `field?` slot admits
three states (present, absent and null, as FR-015-AC-70 draws them), so reading one is refused as
`NullablePresence`. Drawing the null state is a later slice and is not planned here.

The refusal is one more arm of the existing `UnsupportedObligation`, beside
`V2ClauseOperatorNotSupported` and `NoFiniteEncoding`; it is not a parallel family. Composite
equality over option operands (FR-015-AC-69 to FR-015-AC-71, IR-264) is a different family,
`composite_equality`, and is untouched.

### Semantic authority

QSL and the Contract IR own option definedness: a read of `quire.op.option.value` is admitted
only where a `Present` fact guards it, so an admitted package never carries an unguarded read
(IR `OptionPresence` definedness). quire-exact owns the `Undefined::NoneValue` reason, which only
a direct evaluation of an unlinked expression can meet. FR-014 states no option operation. This
requirement therefore owns the one CG-side rule that neither states: the generated clause oracle
reads `option.present` as the presence leaf, reads `option.value` as the payload leaf, and maps
`NoneValue` for an absent read to the thin `ClauseStop::Undefined` of FR-015-AC-145.

### Tractability measurement (IR-1448 probe)

The encoding was probed before it was specified, as a hand-written scratch crate with installed
Kani 0.68.0 following the IR-1022 recipe: a native presence `bool` and payload `i64` in a `Copy`
struct, a `Copy` payload-free stop and no heap-carrying enum arm. The probe, its limits and its
verdicts are recorded in `docs/qualification/ir1448-kani-option-probe.md`. It measures the
encoding, not CG generation: no harness was generated by CG and no criterion below is
implemented. Every probed form verified or was falsified in under one second, so no refusal
below is a cost refusal.

## Inputs

- An admitted V2 clause claim (FR-015-AC-40) whose body reads an operand of checked type
  `Option<T>` as above, through `quire.op.option.present` or `quire.op.option.value`.
- The clause's other operations, as FR-015-AC-40 and FR-015-AC-41 admit them.
- The proof subject, backend and ceilings of FR-015, [FR-025](./FR-025-generated-subject-abi.md)
  and [FR-028](./FR-028-bounded-proof-ceilings.md).

## Outputs

- A supported clause: its harness, symbolic-input correspondence (presence leaf, then payload
  leaves in ascending path order) and source-associated identity record.
- A refused clause: `Unsupported(OptionNotLowered { node_id, reason })` and no harness, with
  `reason` one of `PayloadNotScalar`, `NestedOption`, `NullablePresence` or
  `OptionOperationNotLowered`, each retaining the node and the offending family or operation.

## Behavior

### Draw

When a clause reads an option operand of a supported shape, the harness SHALL draw one presence
`bool` and, for each payload leaf, one native `i64` or `bool`.

The drawn values SHALL be plain `Copy` values with no allocation and no heap-carrying enum arm.

When the presence draw is false, the harness SHALL constrain every payload leaf to its canonical
value, `0` for an integer leaf and `false` for a Boolean leaf.

When the presence draw is true, the harness SHALL constrain each integer leaf inside its declared
bound by the rule of FR-015-AC-11.

### Operations

When a clause applies `quire.op.option.present`, the oracle SHALL return the presence draw.

When a clause applies `quire.op.option.value` to a present option, the oracle SHALL return the
payload, and a composite payload is projected leaf by leaf through the clause's own field or
position reads.

When a clause applies `quire.op.option.value` to an absent option, the oracle SHALL stop with
`ClauseStop::Undefined`.

The harness SHALL NOT substitute a default value or a canonical payload for an absent read.

The harness SHALL NOT add an assumption that excludes the absent draw.

When a clause guards `quire.op.option.value` with `quire.op.option.present` through a
short-circuit connective (`implies`, `and`, `or`), the oracle SHALL evaluate the guarded operand
only when the connective's left operand requires it, as the scalar clause oracle does for
`implies`.

When a clause guards `quire.op.option.value` as above and the option is absent, the harness
SHALL complete the oracle without a stop.

An admitted clause never carries an unguarded read, so the absent stop is a defensive path of the
generated oracle. It is exercised on a direct fixture outside admission (FR-054-AC-5), as
FR-015-AC-150 exercises the out-of-domain stop on a constructed refusal.

### Refusal

When an option operand's payload has any leaf whose family is not Boolean or a bounded integer,
CG SHALL refuse the clause as `OptionNotLowered` with reason `PayloadNotScalar` naming the leaf
family. The family list is closed by that rule: text, decimal, rational, float, quantity, enum,
timestamp, uuid, union, population and collection are all `PayloadNotScalar`.

An option of an unbounded integer keeps `requires_bound` (FR-015-AC-61), and an integer payload
bound beyond `i64` keeps its existing bound refusal.

When an option operand is the payload of an option, or any leaf of a record or tuple payload is
itself an option, CG SHALL refuse the clause with reason `NestedOption`.

When a clause reads a record field of optional presence (`field?`), CG SHALL refuse the clause
with reason `NullablePresence`.

When a clause applies a `quire.op.option.*` operation other than `present` and `value` (for
example `quire.op.option.some`), CG SHALL refuse it with reason `OptionOperationNotLowered`
naming the operation, and not as `V2ClauseOperatorNotSupported`.

The refusals SHALL NOT map to `NoFiniteEncoding`, `StateFrameRefused` or
`V2ClauseOperatorNotSupported`.

A refused item SHALL NOT discard a supported sibling item (FR-015-AC-23).

When a clause meets several refusals, CG SHALL report the first one met in a depth-first, left-to-right walk of
the clause condition from its root that follows each reference once, which is the walk the V2
clause classifier already makes for the first unsupported operator and which this requirement
does not change. Among refusals at one node, a shape refusal (`PayloadNotScalar`, `NestedOption`, `NullablePresence`,
`OptionOperationNotLowered`) precedes a bound refusal of the payload's integer leaves, because the
shape decides which leaves exist.

### Evidence

The harness identity record SHALL retain the presence leaf, the payload leaves and the
canonical-absent rule.

When counterexample playback is decoded, CG SHALL group a presence leaf and its payload leaves
into one option witness value.

When counterexample playback holds an absent option with a non-canonical payload leaf, CG SHALL
refuse the playback.
