## Verdict

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FND-001 | medium | No-result scalar postconditions bypass the effective-signature read and typed unit-return ABI. `lower` invokes `read_result_signature` only after finding a `result` binder, then routes all other postconditions through `render_postcondition`; that renderer calls the subject into an untyped binding with its one read parameter and never validates the anchor's complete binder order. Thus an admitted no-result operation with a different parameter list can produce a malformed subject call instead of a typed refusal, and no-result subjects are not required to return `()`. | src/kani/generate/clause/v2.rs:97 |
