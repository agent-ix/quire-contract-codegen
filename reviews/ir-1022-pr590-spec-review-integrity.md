## Summary

Independent Claude Opus 5.5 review of CG PR #590 (Ticket: IR-1022) at 1e3c2936b997e047198791674400f7224e096295, base 4788b46723d7454af86fdbe9c7b4f4ad1154cbb6. This review checks AD-007's internal consistency and its consistency with FR-015-AC-39, AC-41, AC-47, AC-48, AC-119 and AC-125, FR-016-AC-8, FR-017-AC-60, AD-003 and AD-006. The proposal keeps all 1001 valuations: one draw, a lossless injective and surjective map onto [0,1000], and the same binding fed to the oracle, native expectation, subject and cover. The AD-003 semantic obligation identity is kept apart from the harness record, and regeneration stays byte-identical.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FND-001 | medium | FR-015-AC-39 requires an inclusive assumption equal to the input's declared domain. AD-007 proposes "assume its value is at most 1000", leaving the type to supply the lower bound. It does not list AC-39 as affected. Under the generic rule, a domain equal to a carrier's full range, such as [0,255] in u8 or [-128,127] in i8, would get no assumption at all. AD-007 should list AC-39 among the conflicts or require both checked endpoints to be assumed literally in every case. | spec/assurance/AD-007-v2-symbolic-width-tractability.md:68 |
| FND-002 | low | The generic rule picks "the narrowest supported signed or unsigned primitive" containing both endpoints, but gives no tie-break between equal-width carriers. For [0,1000], both i16 and u16 qualify, yet the text names u16. Generated bytes, harness identity and witness width depend on this choice, so the rule must say which carrier wins, for example unsigned when the minimum is at least 0. | spec/assurance/AD-007-v2-symbolic-width-tractability.md:65 |

## Verdict

Changes requested. FND-001 adds an unsurfaced AC-39 conflict to the owner decision. FND-002 is a determinism gap to close before the rule is made generic.
