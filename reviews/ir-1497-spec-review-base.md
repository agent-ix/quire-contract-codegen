## Summary

Ticket: IR-1497 (related IR-1439). Spec-only PR #773 (head b58d99bc, one commit on 67739cf5): FR-050 draws exact quantity magnitudes from the carried_quantity leaf of a convert<Quantity<u>> operand and composes the conversion into the operation proof; new FR-050-AC-11..14, FR-025-AC-18 and carried-leaf ABI row, TC-064 steps 10-12, TC-036 step 18, tests.md, spec.md row 96, TC-030 row. The decision itself (language unchanged, bounded_quantity is a dense interval, carried_quantity gives the finite domain) is not re-litigated; this review checks that the spec implements it correctly. Checked against: quire-semantic-value origin/main src/quantity.rs (convert_quantity, convert, QuantityTarget::Exact, Conversion::identified_quantity, evaluate_quantity_unit, compare_quantity); CG src/oracle/scalar/render.rs and lower.rs (QuantityConversion body calls quantity_owner = quire_semantic_value::quantity; check_operand); CG src/kani/generate/negotiate.rs and outcome.rs (RequiresBound, MissingBound, NoFiniteEncoding); Contract IR origin/main 7add9a5 operations.rs (check_bounded_dimensions, check_quantity_conversion, check_operands carried-operand refusal, SameDimension not enforced); Contract IR FR-038-AC-73/248/471/485/486; QSpec FR-142-AC-27..31 and FR-154 'Unit bounds'; CG FR-014-AC-21..25, 48..52, 59..68; FR-046-AC-2, FR-051-AC-2/AC-5; FR-028; Linear IR-1443 (comment of 2026-11-10).

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Verified correct: carried operands are refused by the reader (operations.rs check_operands) and the language (QSpec FR-142-AC-27/31, FR-497-AC-2); check_bounded_dimensions only applies to add/convert/lt when a bounded_quantity is involved; FR-038-AC-73/471 make bare unit parameters requires_bound; FR-038-AC-485/486 admit bounded_quantity as a rational interval; FR-014-AC-66 accepts a carried source to convert; the composed native shape is implementable with QSV's public convert_quantity(QuantityTarget::Exact) (owner is quire-semantic-value, re-exported as quantity_owner in generated code) and evaluate_quantity_unit / compare_quantity; QSV's Exact conversion has no inexact or out-of-domain refusal path (only charges, so Incomplete) and refuses a dimension mismatch as IllTyped before any charge; AC-13's Int/Decimal/Rational bindings and per-component fit (Decimal lifted bound, Rational canonical) match FR-025/FR-046/FR-051; FR-025-AC-18 sits inside the AC table with a unique number; counts 'fourteen' / 'AC-1 through AC-14' are updated in FR-050 Status, TC-064 Status and tests.md; TC-036 step 18 and expected result 18 match FR-025-AC-18; spec.md row 96 and the TC-030 row are consistent; FR-049/051/052/053 lines are untouched. Defects: AC-12's reachability, zero-charge claim and citation; AC-1's stale carried-result acceptance; the half-applied P2 (AC-6/Status/TC-064 step 5 vs AC-5); the FR-025 carried row's Decimal fit rule; the FR-154 fallback wording.

| FND-001 | medium | AC-12 says an IllTyped conversion is 'reachable only for a hand-built dimension mismatch at CG's defensive seam'. The Contract IR reader does not check dimensions for a carried-to-exact convert. check_quantity_conversion returns None when the result is neither carried nor bounded, check_bounded_dimensions returns None when no operand or result is bounded_quantity, and SameDimension is listed among the catalogue constraints 'this reader enforces none of' (operations.rs, origin/main 7add9a5). Only genuine QSL source refuses it (QSpec FR-142-AC-27). So a hand-built package that the reader admits reaches this case without any private seam. Say 'reachable only from a hand-built package; QSL refuses it upstream' and let TC-064 step 10 use a reader-admitted package. The zero-charge rule for an exact target is FR-014-AC-49. | FND-002 | medium | AC-12 asserts an IllTyped conversion stop 'with zero charges and unchanged counters'. The Behavior evaluates the conversions in operand order on one Meter.
