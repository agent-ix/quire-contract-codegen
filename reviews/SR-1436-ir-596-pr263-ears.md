# SR-1436: CG PR 263 EARS conformance: FR-031 behaviour statements

## Summary

Ticket: IR-596. The FR-031 Behavior bullets and the 11 ACs were read against the EARS patterns. `quire validate` reports no EARS diagnostic for FR-031; its only warnings are the 2 pre-existing FR-017 lines.

What conforms:

- Arithmetic bullets 1-3 are ubiquitous with the generator as subject.
- The `saturate` bullet is a correct unwanted-behaviour (If/then) statement.
- The consumer bullets name their subjects and use "shall".
- The V1 bundle bullet uses "shall".
- Every AC is declarative and observable.

## Verdict

Conformant apart from one low finding: several normative bullets state behaviour as fact without "shall".
