## Verdict

CHANGES SUGGESTED. Outcome reachability matches the owner: comparisons produce only Completed/Incomplete; arithmetic produces no Undefined (Undefined::IeeeNotFinite is to_exact only); Refused(IeeeNotExact) only on the finite path under exact; DistinctIeeeWidths and IeeeWithExactOperand are unreachable with fixed IEEE widths; division by zero and invalid operations complete with their flag under every mode including exact; the bit-pattern draw is a bijection (float64 via i64-as-u64, float32 constrained to 0..=0xFFFF_FFFF) and the decoder's no-checked-conversion rule keeps negative i64 patterns; every typed control is observable in a generated harness. One gap in the stop enumeration.

| FND-001 | medium | The Incomplete limit kinds are listed as 'integer_bits or value_occurrences at ieee.operands, integer_bits at the two arithmetic points and result_units at ieee.result-retain'.
