# SR-1292: CG PR 250 EARS conformance

## Summary

Ticket: IR-543. PR: agent-ix/quire-contract-codegen#250 at 2995b8d. I checked each new
requirement sentence against the EARS patterns and checked that the ACs are direct assertions.

Clean:

- Statement bullet 1 is a well-formed ubiquitous "shall".
- The FR-022 sentence is a well-formed unwanted-behaviour sentence ("If FR-015 reports ...
  then the generator shall refuse ...").
- The FR-021 addition is a ubiquitous "shall" (its content is in SR-1291 FND-003).
- The five ACs are direct, checkable assertions with no "shall" and no tracker state.
- The 🚧 planned markers sit only in the matrix rows. The one ticket reference (IR-543) is in the
  matrix status note, which follows the repo's precedent (FR-015-AC-43 "PLANNED (IR-489)").

## Verdict

The form is sound. There are two low findings, on keyword choice and on bullets that state no
system behaviour.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FND-001 | low | Statement bullets 2 and 3 open with `Where` ("Where an earlier pass guarantees a condition", "Where a condition cannot be expressed that way"). In EARS, `Where` introduces an optional feature, not a condition. Bullet 3 is an unwanted-behaviour case and should read "If a condition cannot be expressed that way, then the code shall return ...". Bullet 2 is a design rule. It reads better as a ubiquitous statement ("The code shall use a guarantee an earlier pass establishes by ...") | spec/core/non-functional/NFR-005-no-generation-panics.md:25-30 |
| FND-002 | low | Bullets 4 and 5 state no system behaviour. "The generator shall keep FR-014-AC-39, FR-018-AC-19 and FR-021-AC-21 as the stricter scans" is a rule about the spec. "The generator shall add no new public error type ...: the one new variant it needs is `RoutedGenerationError::KaniRecordCountMismatch`" is a plan for the change, and it goes stale once the change lands. No AC verifies either. Move both to Rationale or Scope as notes, and keep the variant in FR-022 and interface-001, where it belongs (see SR-1291 FND-001) | spec/core/non-functional/NFR-005-no-generation-panics.md:31-36 |
