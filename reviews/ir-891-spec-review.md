## Findings

| FND-001 | high | FR-016-AC-6's "absent limit refused before witness data is read" contradicts FR-016-AC-1 ("Every retained witness decodes into typed values or is refused as malformed", marked Covered) and the Behavior partition ("classify every witness into exactly one of the outcomes"): the missing-limit refusal is neither decoded nor malformed, has no typed code, and is not in the partition. ## Verdict

**CHANGES REQUESTED.** The direction (input-carried identity, caller-supplied limits, no invented
number, no pin) is sound and the recommendation is right. FND-001 (high) must be fixed before merge;
FND-002 to FND-005 should be fixed in this PR. Merge sequencing with PR #457 is in SR-13292.
