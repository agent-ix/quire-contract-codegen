# SR-1453: CG PR 267 spec review (base)

## Summary

Ticket: IR-465. Base checklist over the diff, with the code facts re-measured: CG `src/replay/`
at the PR head (the diff is spec-only, so `src/` equals `origin/main`), QSL `qsl-replay` at the
revision CG's `Cargo.lock` resolves and at QSL `origin/main`, and Contract IR `src/kani/outcome.rs`
at the revision CG's lock resolves.

Verified true:

- `ReplayInputs::admit` sorts the dependencies by identity, pre-checks a repeated identity and
  returns `DependencyLockError::Duplicate`, then builds `DependencyInput::new(libraries)` and maps
  its error to `Input`. It is called only from `ReplayPackage::new` and the frame replay path, both
  after Kani refuted.
- QSL `DependencyInput::new` (merged, present at CG's locked revision) refuses a second library
  under one identity as `DuplicateIdentity`, `code()` `invalid_package`, `cause()`
  `conflicting-definition`. The cause name is right. "Buildable today" holds: deleting the
  pre-check and the variant needs no pending QSL type.
- The CG-defect list of FR-029-AC-11 plus the setup-refusal, fault and `Refused`-wrapper readings
  still partition every CG-raised replay failure once `Duplicate` is gone
  (`SpineReplayError`, `FrameReplayError`, `ReplayPackageError`, `DependencyLockError::Input`).
- No held or totality-exception wording about `Duplicate` remains in `spec/`; remaining
  `held`/`interim`/`NonZero`/`option B` hits are unrelated (FR-031, AD-002, the vacuity ruling).
- AC ids are unchanged; no id was minted. TC-040 steps 12 and 13 and TC-041 steps 11 and 12 match
  the new rule; the Status step lists and the two `tests.md` rows agree with FR-029 and FR-030
  Status.
- Every QSL ruling the diff states is marked as relayed on IR-465. No SHAs, pins, line citations
  or compatibility wording were added.
- `make spec` passes with the known warnings only (module registry duplicates and FR-017 line 152
  EARS warnings). `quire coverage --strict --scope .` reports 91 unbacked rows on both
  `origin/main` and the PR head; the only differences are line-number shifts in FR-029 and FR-030.
  No "no archetype registered" diagnostic appeared.

## Verdict

Not mergeable as is: one high finding. The relayed `Declined` code rule (FR-030-AC-2 and its
Behavior bullet) names code sources that Contract IR's `KaniOutcome`, the map's input, does not
carry. The `Duplicate` half of the PR is accurate.
