Extend a reader-admitted function package fixture to make K items and R functions, meter visits through `generate_exact_function_oracles`, and record all nine cells after the #513 rebase.

The ticket specifically asks for dispositions, occurrences, bytes, refusals and consumed work, plus a refused sibling and multiple authorities. Record and compare those outputs in a focused fixture or a measured before/after report before claiming the IR-988 function slice satisfies acceptance check 3.
