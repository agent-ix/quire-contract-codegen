## Summary

Ticket: IR-1022. Spec-only PR #781 (head 9c35b4fb, one commit on base dbb88103). It accepts an exhaustive concrete enumeration over the 1001 points of the Int[0,1000] clause as evidence in place of the Inconclusive/TimedOut symbolic Kani proof. It adds FR-015-AC-132 (the enumeration) and FR-017-AC-64 (its evidence record, with no terminal value) and a new FR-017 Behavior statement. It amends FR-015-AC-41, AC-119 and AC-126, FR-017-AC-60 and AC-63, AD-007, TC-025 (step 51), TC-027 and tests.md. Upgrade: IR-1509. Reviewed against CG source on base (src/kani/terminal.rs, src/kani/generate/clause/v2.rs, src/oracle/clause.rs, src/oracle/clause/native.rs, src/oracle/scalar/render.rs, tests/it/kani_obligations_state_frame.rs), open PR #583 (installed mutant controls), FR-029/FR-030, Linear IR-1022 and IR-1509, `make spec` and quire matrix. No cargo or Kani was run.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Checked: what is visited (0..=1000 in order, no sampling, no early exit on success) and what is computed at each value (subject r, embedded oracle with result bound to r, the test's own i128 expectation, kernel probe). Identity confusion: AC-132 says the test calls neither the oracle nor the helper for (c), which keeps (c) independent. Domain edges: 0 and 1000 are both visited; at 1000 the antecedent is false and the consequent probe is guarded off (v2.rs probe guard). Terminal purity: the enumeration builds no KaniRunOutcome. Defects: the enumeration does not execute the generated harness it stands in for; the retained playback refers to a run that may not exist; and the PR states an outcome for unmeasured runs.

| FND-001 | medium | What the enumeration executes is not the harness whose symbolic proof it stands in for. (c) and (d) are re-stated by the test ('evaluated by the test itself'; '(the oracle's outcome of amount + 1 against amount_current + 1 in i128)'), and the generated proof body (assumption, generated native expectation, probes and their antecedent guards, the completed/refused-domain assertion, the result-binder wiring, the final cover) is never run. AC-41 itself says that 'substituting a hand-restated assertion for the generated one is insufficient' for the playback. Under the ruling, a generated-harness defect (a wrong probe guard, a missing subject-result comparison, a wrong binder) would survive the enumeration and be caught only by the two AC-60 playbacks, at 999 and at the healthy input. Running the sealed proof body per value is feasible: #583 already turns the generated harness into a host #[test] by replacing #[kani::proof] (tests/it/kani_obligations_state_frame.rs:957), and FR-017-AC-57 appends a playback test. Fix: either evaluate the sealed generated proof body at each of the 1001 values with deterministic inputs, or state explicitly that the generated harness's own assertions are covered only by the AC-60 playbacks and that the enumeration covers subject, oracle and kernel only. | spec/kani/functional/FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md:938 |

Fix: say 'stay unmet; the healthy and exact-999 runs were measured Inconclusive TimedOut; the other mutant runs are unmeasured'. | spec/kani/functional/FR-015-bounded-kani-obligations.md:853 |
