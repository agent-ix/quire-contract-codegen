# Integrity review of the numeric/state strategy slice

## Summary

Reviewed `agent-e/codegen-3-numeric-strategies-spec` at `6f73e2d` against base `240fad8`. The review
checked internal consistency across FR-008 through FR-013, NFR-004, and TC-017 through TC-022. It also
checked them against FR-001, FR-002, NFR-002, the interface-001 `harness_strategy_slice`,
`diagnostics`, `identity_envelope`, and new `bound_strategy_slice` keys, the test matrix, the index,
and the repository `CLAUDE.md`.

Checks that passed:

- FR-010-AC-1 was recomputed from FR-010's own rules for `amount < 7` over 0..=1000. The domain edges
  are -1, 0, 1, 999, 1000, 1001 and the literal edges are 6, 7, 8. That gives nine cases, with tags
  equal to the AC.
- FR-010-AC-2 was recomputed with `Pre` as the first read. Pre 0 gives {0 H, 1 V, -1 OOD, 1001 OOD}.
  Pre 1 gives {1 H, 0 V, 2 V, -1 OOD, 1001 OOD}. Pre 999 is symmetric to pre 1, and pre 1000 to
  pre 0. The out-of-domain first values give (-1, 0) and (1001, 1000). That is 20 cases, equal to
  the AC and inside NFR-004's 40-case target. The AC only holds under an ordering the FR never
  states (FND-21003).
- TC-019 step 3's `amount <= 1000` census holds only `Holds` cases in domain, so its refusal is
  consistent.
- Every `relationships` target exists.
- Test-matrix rows match each FR's AC set, and FR-013-AC-5 has its Inspection row.
- The `bound_strategy_slice` refusal list, tag set, verdict mapping, and rate shape match the FRs.
- `--backend none` and one `ProofAttestationV1` per artifact agree with `identity_envelope`.
- The skipped FR-007, NFR-003, and TC-014 through TC-016 are explained in `spec/index.md`. TC-011
  is explained in `CLAUDE.md`.

The main defect: the slice requires zero proptest global rejects, but under interface-001 a
`Pre`-anchored `Violated` case is itself a global reject. Most other findings are unassigned
vocabulary: terminal states, read ordering, Boolean reads, and the form of the census record.

| FND-21001 | high | NFR-004 Statement and its global-reject metric, FR-009-AC-3, TC-018 step 2, and TC-020 step 5 require zero proptest global rejects for 10,000-case campaigns over `amount < 7`. The existing adapter returns `TestCaseError::reject` for it. So any `Violating` or `Broad` campaign on a `Pre` anchor has global rejects. At the caller's `max_global_rejects` it can also end `Exhausted`, which contradicts NFR-004's zero-`Exhausted` metric.
