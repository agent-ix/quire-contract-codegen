AD-004 amendment (commit c7d6517: one sentence of the `kani/generate/` import rule, and
`"frame"` added to negotiate's list in tests/it/layout.rs):

- Correct against step 4d. Step 4d (AD-004:740-742) says the frame family is ported "with

  pass in `make ci`.

  requirement, and the normative decision (negotiate calls the frame role function) was
  already made and reviewed in FR-015. The edit is a conforming update of an enumeration that
  PR #275 should have made, not a new architecture decision. It sits in its own commit with a
  clear message. Recommendation: accept, and note in IR-461 that PR #275's spec lane missed
  the AD-004 list.
