## Summary

Ticket: IR-1442. Spec-only PR #769 (head 098be30e, one commit on origin/main 24a73c74) adding FR-053 and TC-067, the planned Kani row for the three IEEE comparisons and float32/float64 add, sub, mul and div (sqrt and fma conditional on P3), with edits to FR-025 (float ABI row, AC-7, new AC-17), FR-028, TC-036 step 17, TC-030 IEEE-float row, spec/kani/matrix/tests.md and spec/spec.md. Reviewed against quire-exact 5db2d5e3 (CG Cargo.lock pin) src/ieee.rs and src/accounting{,/units}.rs, CG src/oracle/scalar/{derive,derive/tests}.rs and src/kani/generate/negotiate.rs, Contract IR origin/main 7add9a5 (lower.rs requires_bound, FR-038-AC-8), QSpec proposals/checked-package-v2/schema.json operation catalogue, qsl-replay 434869e WitnessValue, Linear IR-69 (planner ruling comment, Option A), IR-1446, IR-1502.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Verified against quire-exact 5db2d5e3 (CG Cargo.lock pin) src/ieee.rs and src/accounting{,/units}.rs, CG src/oracle/scalar/{derive,derive/tests}.rs and src/kani/generate/negotiate.rs, Contract IR origin/main 7add9a5 (lower.rs requires_bound, FR-038-AC-8), QSpec proposals/checked-package-v2/schema.json operation catalogue, qsl-replay 434869e WitnessValue, Linear IR-69 (planner ruling comment, Option A), IR-1446, IR-1502. Correct: the derivable list (FR-014 add/sub/mul/div at both widths, the three comparisons, to_float32/64) and the sqrt/fma/from_exact/to_rational refusals match derive.rs and the TC-024 refused-identity test; the operand-swap claims (add/mul only via the leftmost-NaN payload, numeric_equal/bit_identical swaps equivalent and unclaimed, fma (a,b) only via NaN) match classify(); division by zero completes with a signed infinity and divide_by_zero and every invalid operation completes with the canonical quiet NaN and invalid (plan_divide/plan_add/plan_multiply/plan_square_root/plan_fma); IeeeNotExact is raised only under exact, after ieee.round and before ieee.result-retain (finish_rounding); NaN precedes the invalid-operation test; the charge order and amounts match arithmetic()/compare(); the T1-T4 table, the ten pair claims for add, M1 (both widths, even lower neighbour), ONE/THREE at binary32 and FIVE/THREE at binary64, the fma single-rounding points and the overflow/underflow points were recomputed and hold; total_order mutants (f),(g),(h),(i),(j),(l),(m) are distinguishing against total_order_key; IR-69 is not implemented on Contract IR 7add9a5 (requires_bound false for Float32/Float64, FR-038-AC-8 eight forms); the tractability facts (compare_ieee has no BigInt; evaluate_ieee uses BigInt/BigUint, wide alignment shifts and a Newton integer_square_root loop) are accurate; WitnessValue::Float32(u32)/Float64(u64) exist; the branch is one commit on origin/main and mergeable, with one row per family (FR-049..053) in tests.md, spec.md, FR-028 and TC-030. Defects: the prerequisite that is meant to tie the proof to the shipped owner is not carried by its ticket, the arithmetic proof compares the carrier with itself, and a tractability figure is understated.
