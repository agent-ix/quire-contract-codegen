## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Jev (typesafe.ai System One) is not installed here, so this analysis was done by hand. Distinguishing as written: AC-63(a) catches swapped vectors (argv per launch) and a missing witness launch (launch count). (b) first case catches classify-at-proof-phase, because the mutant yields Falsified or FailedWithoutCounterexample, never WitnessDisagreed. (c) catches a retry through 'no third launch'. (d) and (f) catch a witness launch after an exhausted bound, Verified, CoverUnsatisfied or NoVerdict through launch counts and the no-concrete-playback argv check. (e) catches whole-batch relaunch. FR-028-AC-23/TC-039 step 21 can fail: a verified run that records a witness vector fails it. Defects: three named mutant controls can survive their stated cases.

| FND-004 | high | AC-63(c) gets the 1.5x ceiling case backwards. A witness stand-in that finishes at 1.5 times T or M with a verifying report is killed at T or M when the ceilings are unchanged, which is the correct behaviour. TC-027 has it the right way round ('so a raised ceiling would yield WitnessDisagreed') but states no expected outcome. Fix: say that the 1.5x stand-ins are TimedOut and MemoryExhausted with recorded ceilings equal to the proof launch's, and that WitnessDisagreed there means the raise-ceiling mutant. State the same expected outcome in TC-027. | spec/kani/functional/FR-017-kani-execution-evidence.md:AC-63 row (c) |
