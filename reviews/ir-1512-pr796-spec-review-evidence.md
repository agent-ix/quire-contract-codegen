No cargo or build was run. `quire validate` over spec, plan and reviews (the make spec target) reports the same 6 warnings on the branch as on origin/main. `quire matrix` lists FR-024-AC-69 and FR-024-AC-70 as untagged and FR-024-AC-58 as tagged by tests/it/state_clause_invariant_replay.rs:297. Method: spec-review/evidence.

## Verdict

**MINOR CHANGES.** Step 40's rewrite has two mutants that a test can fail: omitting the unread field ends `Refused`, not `MissingField`, and an invented value settles. Step 49's range controls (0 and 1000 accepted; 1001 and -1 refused) and the narrowed-range fixture in step 48 tell an accessor-range check from an `i64` check. The step 48 value-swap control (7 vs 3) can fail. The order mutants are covered under spec-review/base FND-001. Mutant sites and one mutant are missing.
