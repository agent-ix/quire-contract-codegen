## Summary

Ticket: IR-631. Spec-only PR #777 (head da8e03e5, one commit on origin/main f38f8024), reviewed in a detached worktree of that head, scoped to git diff origin/main...HEAD (6 files). Checked against origin/main source: src/replay/scalar_builder.rs (ScalarBuildError's 11 variants, settle(&ScalarSentRequest, Option<&OperatorParityReport>) -> Result<qsl_replay::TerminalValue, ScalarBuildError>, plan/complete), src/kani/terminal.rs (ReplaySettlement's six variants, TerminalPairError, run_terminal_value, ir_outcome_terminal_value), src/replay/composite_builder.rs (OriginalReplayContext::new, check_recompiled_context), src/kani/identity.rs, src/core/scalar_authority.rs, tests/it/scalar_replay_binding.rs, scalar_replay_residuals.rs, scalar_playback.rs, scalar_literal_operands.rs, and qsl-replay at the locked rev 434869e (scalar.rs OperatorParityResult and terminal_value, execute/operator_parity.rs, proof_result.rs from_replay_refusal, bounds.rs) plus quire-exact 5db2d5e (numeric.rs, accounting.rs). make spec and quire matrix pass at the PR head; no grammar warnings in the changed files. No cargo and no Kani were run. Failure domain: what each ScalarBuildError means for a genuinely falsified run, identity confusion between converter and pairing, and the order of refusals relative to the QSL call.

## Verdict

PASS WITH TWO MEDIUMS. Using a distinct ScalarSettlement correctly makes Reproduced and Inconclusive(ReplayParity) unrepresentable on this route, and settlement keeps settle's SentContext-then-Report checks. The open questions are which internal CG failures must still yield a terminal record, and the unchecked tie between a settlement and the Kani evidence it is paired with.
