## Summary

Ticket: IR-1244. Spec-only PR #770 (head 154396ea, one commit on base 67739cf5) for IR-1436 decision D5: the oracle-function Kani harness runs over pre-decoded prepared data that a host differential binds to the reader. It edits FR-021 Behavior, adds FR-021-AC-44/45/46 and their mutation rows, and edits FR-047-AC-1, TC-031 steps 15/16, TC-061 step 2 and both tests.md files. Reviewed against CG origin/main 67739cf5 src/oracle/function/owner_render.rs (HEADER PreparedFunction::new/ready/finish, render_item, call_item), src/oracle/function/owner_path.rs (CheckedFunctionResources, apply_checked_function, select_checked_callable, checked_function_declarations, package_bytes), src/oracle/function/mod.rs generate_exact_function_oracles, src/oracle/claim.rs OracleGenerationError; quire-semantic-value 3c4616d declaration.rs (TypeEnvironment, PartialEq impl, EnvironmentFailure, bounded_with_cancel, seal_union_verified) and location.rs (ExpressionLocation); quire-exact 5db2d5e node.rs (NodeKey), value.rs (UnionMember), value_type.rs, cancel.rs; qsl-replay 434869e application.rs (NominalDeclaration, NominalDeclarationMember); Linear IR-1244 (AC-4 measurements) and IR-1436 D5 decision comment.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Correct and verified: the in-proof/pre-decoded split names the right calls (TypeEnvironment::bounded_with_cancel, seal_union_verified per union, ready/finish with admits) exactly as PreparedFunction::new/ready/finish use them; the 'reader alone exceeds 16 GiB' statement matches the IR-1244 measurement (minimal reader-only crate, 1,006,535 pretty-name entries, oom-kill at 16 GiB); the 16 GiB fit of the cut is not claimed; AC-42 is left unchanged and picks up the Behavior change by reference, consistent with D5. The decision is implemented in spirit, but the spec attributes the pre-decoded data to the reader when the code takes it from the QSL acquisition and callable selection, which the generator does not have; and one sentence overstates what the differential carries.

| FND-001 | high | The pre-decoded data is attributed to the reader, but it is not reader output. PreparedFunction::new (owner_render.rs HEADER) takes the declaration views from checked_function_declarations(declaring_package, acquired, selection) (owner_path.rs:405), which needs the QSL CheckedClosureCompilation from acquire_checked_closure over caller-supplied source bytes, packages, dependencies and limits, and takes parameters/result from selection.signature() (QSL CallableSelection). The public generator generate_exact_function_oracles(source: &CheckedPackageV2, package, requested, ...) (mod.rs:2847) receives neither source bytes nor an acquisition. So 'const data that CG generated from the retained package after the strict reader returned Admitted' cannot be built as written, and Behavior's 'holding only the reader's outputs consumed by PreparedFunction::new' is false. State that the data is derived at generation time from a genuine QSL acquisition and callable selection over the retained source, and name that new generator input. Make a generation-time acquisition, selection or checked_function_declarations refusal a typed generation refusal with no harness (only a reader refusal is named now). Say that the differential binds the data to the reader and to the acquisition/selection outputs. | spec/oracle/functional/FR-021-function-application-oracles.md:537 |
| FND-002 | low | 'Reader decode and admission earn no Kani proof credit; the host differential carries them.' The differential runs the unmodified reader and requires Admitted; it does not verify reader decode or admission, which stay with Contract IR FR-039 evidence. 'Carries them' reads as moving reader assurance into the differential. Say instead that the differential binds the const data to the reader's admitted output, and that reader correctness is owned by FR-039. | spec/oracle/functional/FR-021-function-application-oracles.md:377 |
