## Summary

Ticket: IR-1440. Spec-only PR #766 (head 6c9302b6) adding FR-051 and TC-065, the planned Kani row for decimal scalar operations, with edits to FR-025, FR-028, TC-036, TC-030, spec/kani/matrix/tests.md and spec/spec.md. Reviewed against origin/main 66e7eac3 (includes #764 FR-049 and #765 FR-050), quire-exact decimal.rs/numeric.rs/outcome.rs/accounting.rs, CG src/oracle/scalar/derive.rs and src/kani, qsl-replay WitnessValue, and Contract IR FR-038.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Verified against quire-exact 5db2d5e (unchanged at origin/main 4fe7df6 for decimal.rs, numeric.rs, outcome.rs) and CG src/oracle/scalar/derive.rs: the operator list (decimal add/sub/mul/div/negate, numeric.convert_rounding into a decimal, decimal lt/le/gt/ge) matches the derivable set; decimal.eq/ne are FR-014 OperationNotDerivable; every named outcome (Undefined(DivisionByZero), Refused(InexactDecimal), Refused(DecimalOutOfDomain), Incomplete) and every charge point (decimal.operands, decimal.scale-expansion, decimal.arithmetic, decimal.rounding, decimal.result-retain, ordering.operands, ordering.arithmetic, ordering.result-retain) exists and is ordered as stated; the zero-divisor check follows the decimal.operands charge, so the zero-budget control is right; ordering is by normalized value (Decimal::compare); DecimalResult equality is retained representation plus loss; qsl_replay WitnessValue::Decimal{coefficient, scale} exists; TC-030's amended Decimal row is consistent with FR-014 and FR-015; make spec passes. Two defects remain in the FR text itself.
