## Summary

Ticket: IR-1442. Spec-only PR #769 (head 098be30e, one commit on origin/main 24a73c74) adding FR-053 and TC-067, the planned Kani row for the three IEEE comparisons and float32/float64 add, sub, mul and div (sqrt and fma conditional on P3), with edits to FR-025 (float ABI row, AC-7, new AC-17), FR-028, TC-036 step 17, TC-030 IEEE-float row, spec/kani/matrix/tests.md and spec/spec.md. Reviewed against quire-exact 5db2d5e3 (CG Cargo.lock pin) src/ieee.rs and src/accounting{,/units}.rs, CG src/oracle/scalar/{derive,derive/tests}.rs and src/kani/generate/negotiate.rs, Contract IR origin/main 7add9a5 (lower.rs requires_bound, FR-038-AC-8), QSpec proposals/checked-package-v2/schema.json operation catalogue, qsl-replay 434869e WitnessValue, Linear IR-69 (planner ruling comment, Option A), IR-1446, IR-1502.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Jev (typesafe.ai System One) is not available in this environment, so this analysis was done manually against the owner (quire-exact 5db2d5e3 (CG Cargo.lock pin) src/ieee.rs and src/accounting{,/units}.rs, CG src/oracle/scalar/{derive,derive/tests}.rs and src/kani/generate/negotiate.rs, Contract IR origin/main 7add9a5 (lower.rs requires_bound, FR-038-AC-8), QSpec proposals/checked-package-v2/schema.json operation catalogue, qsl-replay 434869e WitnessValue, Linear IR-69 (planner ruling comment, Option A), IR-1446, IR-1502), recomputing each control point. Distinguishing as claimed: AC-2's draw mutants; AC-8 (a),(b),(c),(e)-(j),(l),(m); AC-9 (a),(b),(d)-(k) and (l) (sqrt(FOUR) exact and Completed under exact, sqrt(TWO) inexact); the add/fma T1-T4 pairs; the mul M1 pairs (nearest-even vs toward-zero correctly unclaimed); div ONE/THREE (FIVE/THREE at binary64) with negation; AC-10's charge mutants; AC-11's decode mutants. Defects: one sqrt mutant pair can never fail, the sqrt and sub points do not distinguish what is claimed, the 'division never ties' premise is false, and two AC-8 points cannot catch their mutant.

| FND-001 | high | AC-9(c) claims the rounding-mode substitution for sqrt over sqrt(TWO) and sqrt(FOUR), and the Behavior withholds only nearest-even vs nearest-away for sqrt. But toward-zero vs toward-negative is an equivalent mutant for sqrt on every input: the result is never negative (a negative operand is invalid, sqrt(Z-) is exact), and round_at differs between the two only when negative. That pair can never fail. The stated points are also too weak for the rest: sqrt(FOUR) is exact (no mode differs); sqrt(TWO) at binary32 rounds down under nearest (0x3FB504F3), so nearest-even, nearest-away, toward-zero and toward-negative all agree and only pairs with toward-positive are caught; at binary64 it rounds up under nearest (0x3FF6A09E667F3BCD), so nearest-even/nearest-away/toward-positive agree. Exclude toward-zero vs toward-negative for sqrt, and add a radicand whose root rounds the other way at each width, or list exactly the pairs sqrt(TWO) distinguishes. | spec/kani/functional/FR-053-float-scalar-harnesses.md:310 |
| FND-002 | medium | AC-9(c) applies T1-T4 'for every pair of the five modes' to sub, but T1-T4 are defined only as add operations.
