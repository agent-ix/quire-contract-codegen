## Summary

Ticket: IR-304. Spec-only PR #768 (head de3d7ee1, base origin/main cf10ec78) rewrites FR-022's QSL requirement-record intake, adds the 'Per-occurrence requirement records' and 'Path conditions in the scalar harness' subsections, rewrites AC-26/27/28, adds AC-31..35 and an IR-304 Rationale, and updates the TC-033 procedure for slices (a)/(b) and the routed tests.md row. Verified against CG src/routed/generate.rs, src/kani/generate/{spec,scalar}.rs, src/kani/identity.rs, src/replay/scalar.rs, src/core/contract_package.rs, src/oracle/scalar/lower.rs; IR quire-contract-model c8dca8c (ContractOccurrenceKeyV1, result_context); QSL origin/main ffec6c72 (qsl-semantics check/claims.rs PathGuard/RequirementRecord/key_claims, qsl-route request.rs RequirementItem).

## Verdict

FAIL (high finding). Checked against real code. The IR ContractOccurrenceKeyV1 has no Ord or Serialize (result_context.rs:94-102), and CheckedOccurrenceRole derives both, so the CG mirror RequirementOccurrence is justified and is not a compatibility layer. The new identity fields follow the existing skip_serializing_if pattern of ScalarObligationIdentity (identity.rs:465-494) and carry the key, not a hash. Nothing digests ScalarObligationIdentity outside one test. Placing records on KaniGenerationContext (Copy, holding a reference) keeps both entry points' signatures. RequirementMissing for OwnResult and the AC-26 swap, which reaches ApplicationMismatch at the lower index, are sound and can fail. QSL RequirementItem/RequirementRecord/PathGuard (qsl-route request.rs:787-803, qsl-semantics claims.rs:289-322) match the four projected facts. Defects: the optional requirement on a NotApplicable item, the unstated frame for selected-side comparisons, and the half-applied slice split.
