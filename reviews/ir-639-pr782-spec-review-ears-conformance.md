## Verdict

CHANGES REQUESTED (low only). quire validate reports no new grammar warning for the changed lines; the two FR-017 warnings (lines 392 and 899) are on existing text. The new ubiquitous, event and optional statements are mostly well formed. The defects are statements whose response cannot be observed, or which carry no shall response.
