## Summary

Ticket: IR-631. Spec-only PR #777 (head da8e03e5, one commit on origin/main f38f8024), reviewed in a detached worktree of that head, scoped to git diff origin/main...HEAD (6 files). Checked against origin/main source: src/replay/scalar_builder.rs (ScalarBuildError's 11 variants, settle(&ScalarSentRequest, Option<&OperatorParityReport>) -> Result<qsl_replay::TerminalValue, ScalarBuildError>, plan/complete), src/kani/terminal.rs (ReplaySettlement's six variants, TerminalPairError, run_terminal_value, ir_outcome_terminal_value), src/replay/composite_builder.rs (OriginalReplayContext::new, check_recompiled_context), src/kani/identity.rs, src/core/scalar_authority.rs, tests/it/scalar_replay_binding.rs, scalar_replay_residuals.rs, scalar_playback.rs, scalar_literal_operands.rs, and qsl-replay at the locked rev 434869e (scalar.rs OperatorParityResult and terminal_value, execute/operator_parity.rs, proof_result.rs from_replay_refusal, bounds.rs) plus quire-exact 5db2d5e (numeric.rs, accounting.rs). make spec and quire matrix pass at the PR head; no grammar warnings in the changed files. No cargo and no Kani were run. Criterion strength (manual: Jev is not available here, so each AC-20 to AC-25 mutant in TC-047's mutation table was traced by hand to a concrete input that must make it fail). Verified on origin/main and qsl-replay 434869e that the step-10 inputs exist. Through a completed plan, Completed(7) gives Diverged (x + x is even) and Incomplete/ExecutionFault give GeneratedFault, since complete() does not check the outcome. Passing genuine facade reports for an out-of-range operand value and for limits.work_units = 0 straight to ScalarSettlement::Parity is honest: the operand value and limits are not part of the O-09 preimage, scalar_replay_binding.rs already gets RefusedInput and Incomplete from the real facade this way, and every quire-exact charge costs one work unit. Neither class is reachable through settlement, because plan refuses out-of-domain operands and the retained limits are u64::MAX.

## Verdict

FAIL ON ONE HIGH. The non-fault Refused input of step 10 cannot be built as written, so the AC-21 'read every Refused as Failed' mutant has no killing input. Two mediums follow from the procedure not saying the settlement result must be fed into the pair function. The other mutants (Diverged to Refuted, Agrees to Failed, RefusedInput to Failed, Incomplete to TimedOut, UnexpectedSettlement, Playback/Observation classification, settle kept, KaniObligationIdentity added to plan) are distinguishing, buildable and not equivalent.
